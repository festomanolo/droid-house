Unmatched Features

Built for Performance, Privacy & Speed

No external servers, no subscription fees. DroidHouse runs completely locally on your Wi-Fi network.

Authentic iMessage Bubbles

Incoming and outgoing messages styled with Apple iMessage corner tail nudges, smooth spring animations, and automatic Swahili smart suggestions like "Hongera sana Aiseeee!!".

AeroCast Wireless Display

High FPS low-latency screen mirroring directly from Android to your Mac desktop, perfect for presentations, demos, and media playback.

Wireless File Explorer

Access Android internal storage, SD Card, Pictures, and Downloads over local Wi-Fi. Drag-and-drop files seamlessly without plugging in cables.

Local Phone Backup System

Full offline backup engine. Backup SMS messages, photos, and system configuration directly to your Mac SSD with zero cloud privacy risks.

Instant Clipboard Syncing

Copy text on your Android phone and paste it immediately on Mac. Remote screenshot capture with immediate preview & export.

Zero Cloud & 100% Private

All network communication occurs strictly on your local Wi-Fi router or direct ADB connection. Your personal texts & files never touch external servers.
