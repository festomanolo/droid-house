Spatial Superpowers

A True Apple Magic Trackpad in Your Palm

Engineered with custom non-linear velocity curves, multi-touch gestures, and mechanical notched haptics that match native macOS trackpad physics.

macOS Acceleration Dynamics

Eliminates the sluggish "sticky mouse" feeling of generic remote apps. Implements macOS power-law velocity curves, sub-millisecond precision micro-damping (0.85×), and high-velocity flick boost (up to 3.85×) with 2-sample exponential moving average (EMA) jitter smoothing.

Dynamic Power-Law Curve + EMA Filter

Multi-Touch Gesture Engine

Native low-level gesture recognizer: 1-finger precision cursor pointing, tap to click, and drag. 2-finger natural inertial scrolling and 2-finger tap for right-click. 3-finger upward swipe for Mission Control / App Exposé and downward swipe for Show Desktop.

1-Finger • 2-Finger • 3-Finger Gestures

Tactile Mechanical Scroll Wheel

Docked 3D cylindrical scroll wheel with physical rubber notched treads. Features dynamic perspective compression, cyan LED illumination, and multi-tier physical hardware vibration motor pulses on every ratchet detent tick for realistic tactile feedback.

Physical Mechanical Haptics • Hardware Motor

Worldwide Tailscale WAN Tunnel

Control your Mac from across the world over cellular LTE/5G or remote Wi-Fi. Features zero-config Tailscale tunnel with embedded RFC 1035 UDP MagicDNS resolver fallback for *.ts.net domains, CGNAT traversal, and 4-32 character custom PIN/passphrase protection.

Zero-Config WAN • MagicDNS UDP Resolver

Built for Performance, Privacy & Speed

Zero cloud servers, zero subscription fees. DroidHouse runs completely locally or peer-to-peer on your private network.

Spatial Magic Trackpad

True macOS trackpad physics. 1-finger click/drag, 2-finger natural inertial scrolling and right-click tap, 3-finger Mission Control swipe, and non-linear power-law acceleration curves.

Anywhere Tailscale WAN

Control and monitor your Mac remotely from anywhere across the globe over cellular or remote Wi-Fi. Features native MagicDNS UDP resolution and 4-32 char PIN pairing.

Live Desktop Stream

Stream your entire Mac desktop at 60 FPS to your Android device with interactive zoom pads, pan mode, system shortcut bar, and floating scroll wheel.

Companion Services Hub

Ultra-lightweight embedded Ktor server on port 8080, AeroCast on 8081, background clipboard bridge, and active notification listener mirroring.

Authentic iMessage SMS

Incoming and outgoing messages styled with Apple iMessage corner tail nudges, spring animations, contact avatar roster, and automatic Swahili smart suggestions ("Hongera sana Aiseeee!!").

AeroCast Wireless Display

High FPS low-latency screen mirroring directly from Android to your Mac desktop with CoreAudio playback capture, perfect for presentations, gaming, and demos.

Wireless File Explorer

Access Android internal storage, SD Card, Pictures, and Downloads over local Wi-Fi. Drag-and-drop files seamlessly without plugging in cables.

Local Phone Backup Engine

Full offline backup engine. Backup SMS messages, photos, and system configuration directly to your Mac SSD with zero cloud privacy risks.

Zero Cloud & 100% Private

All network communication occurs strictly on your local Wi-Fi router or direct peer-to-peer encrypted Tailscale tunnel. Your personal texts & files never touch external servers.
